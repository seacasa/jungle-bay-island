# Jungle Bay Island · $BAYLA

Jungle Bay Island is a body of work before it is a token: real, named artists have painted the
island daily for almost five years — hundreds of credited original works, comics, films, and
70+ documented art-contest payouts that predate the token. $BAYLA carries the name of the
island's muse. Creator fees fund the artist collective.

**Dank Memes + Time [DM+T] = Memetic Finance.**

## The mechanism: heat

Holders accrue **heat**: held time counted onchain, per wallet — and per person, when a holder
links their wallets at the island's register. The count starts at the first buy. Nothing to
connect, nothing to sign, nothing to stake: the chain already knows.

Check any wallet's held time (sol or evm): **https://memetics.wtf/frame**
